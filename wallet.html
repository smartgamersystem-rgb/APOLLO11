🛸 AREA 51 – Public Documents

Secret military base in Nevada, officially acknowledged in 2013. Documents show development of aircraft like the U-2, A-12 OXCART, and SR-71, as well as stealth technology tests.

Secrecy was due to the Cold War. UFO sightings were in many cases experimental aircraft. There is no evidence of extraterrestrial contact in official documents.
